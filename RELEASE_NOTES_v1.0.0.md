# Mutant Monster Lab v1.0.0 — Initial Public Release

The lab is open.

**Mutant Monster Lab** adds a new experimental Pokémon research facility to Cinnabar Island for Pokémon Crystal on Gen1Recomp.

Begin with a struggling research operation and fund **five tiers** of increasingly dangerous science. Experiment directly on individual Pokémon, splice unusual traits between specimens, and manage the instability created by repeatedly pushing Pokémon beyond their normal limits.

At Tier 5, the lab's ultimate project becomes operational: the **Holon Collider**.

Every Collider activation selects **three unique weighted phenomena** that can radically alter wild Pokémon. Encounters can become Lv100, receive randomized types or stat profiles, gain persistent randomized learnsets, become shiny, explode, prevent escape, receive bizarre palettes, shuffle species, break the Lv100 limit, or produce Pokémon that mutate into another species every time they level.

The Collider requires **seven in-game days** to recharge. Pokémon caught during an event keep the mutations attached to them even after future Collider activations change the world again.

## v1.0.0 Highlights

- Five-tier laboratory progression
- Persistent individual Pokémon mutations
- Palette, size, fragment, cry, type and stat experimentation
- Fusion instability and catastrophic fallout
- Full Tier 5 Holon Collider activation sequence
- 11 Collider phenomena
- Exactly three unique weighted phenomena per activation
- Seven-day in-game Collider recharge
- Lv255 Limit Break support
- Persistent randomized movesets and learnsets
- Evolution Cascade with mutation inheritance
- Persistent randomized palettes and Prisma shinies
- Wild encounter shuffling
- Cross-mod mutant palette support for PokeSurvive, Angler's Cove Aquarium and Safari Derby
- Mutation persistence through saves, PC storage and supported evolution paths

This is the first public release. If you discover a particularly creative new way to violate Pokémon biology, bug reports are welcome.
