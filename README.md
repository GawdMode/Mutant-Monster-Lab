# Mutant Monster Lab

**Mutant Monster Lab** is an experimental Pokémon modification for Pokémon Crystal on Gen1Recomp.

Hidden away on Cinnabar Island is a research facility dedicated to pushing Pokémon far beyond their natural limits. Splice Pokémon together, manipulate their traits, discover unstable mutations, experiment with forbidden technology, and gradually expand the facility into something increasingly dangerous.

## Features

### Pokémon Splicing

Combine a **Host Pokémon** with a **Donor Pokémon** to create experimental mutations.

Splicing can alter traits such as:

- Colors and palettes
- Stats
- Types
- Moves
- Other unstable characteristics

Repeated experimentation increases instability and can produce increasingly unusual results.

### Lab Progression

The Cinnabar facility can be upgraded through multiple research tiers.

Higher tiers unlock additional experiments, equipment, rewards, and increasingly extreme ways to modify Pokémon.

The Lab keeps track of your progress as you expand its capabilities.

### Holon Collider

At the highest levels of research, the Lab gains access to the **Holon Collider**.

Activating it can produce dramatic mutations that go far beyond normal splicing, including randomized experimental effects and extremely rare discoveries.

The Collider also features its own full-screen activation sequence.

### Gameboy Kid Pokémon Trade

A unique Pokémon can be obtained through a special Lab event and functions as a usable Pokémon at **Facility Tier 3**.

It includes:

- Its classic appearance
- Gen I-inspired cry
- Leveling beyond the normal Lv100 limit, up to Lv255
- Compatibility with every TM/HM
- Ability to change its appearance by speaking to the Gameboy Kid

This Pokémon cannot be used as a normal splicing subject due to its unstable data.

### Experimental Types

Mutations can rarely produce five new experimental types:

- **VOID**
- **SUPER**
- **NUCLEAR**
- and more...

These types have their own defensive properties and use existing Pokémon types as offensive STAB affinities.

They can appear through unstable splicing and Holon Collider experiments.

### Experimental Moves

Seven new moves can emerge from Lab experimentation:

- **RIGAMORTIC**
- **BIORHYTHM**
- **GENESPLICE**
- and more...

These moves use custom effects while remaining visually grounded in Pokémon Crystal's battle system.

Some can appear on sufficiently unstable Pokémon or through Collider experiments.

### Mutant Challengers

At **Facility Tier 4**, visiting trainers begin arriving at the Lab to test their own experiments against yours.

Seven different challengers rotate through the facility based on the in-game day.

Each challenger has:

- A unique trainer identity and dialogue
- A full team of **six Lv100 mutant Pokémon**
- Unique Pokémon across the entire 42-Pokémon challenger roster
- Altered colors, sizes, stats, typings and moves
- Experimental types and moves
- Pokémon with donor body fragments
- Mutants with altered cries

Defeating unique challengers is tracked at the Lab sign.

### Experimental Genes

Each Mutant Challenger has a unique Gene reward.

Your first victory against a challenger awards their Gene. On later appearances, defeating that trainer for the first time that day awards **5 Rare Candies**.

Genes are powerful one-use held items that activate when their holder enters battle:

- **BERSERK GENE** — Attack +2, causes confusion
- **BULWARK GENE** — Defense +2, causes paralysis
- **RAPID GENE** — Speed +2, causes a burn
- and more...

### PokeSurvive Integration

When **PokeSurvive** is also installed, Mutant Monster Lab can trigger the special **GLITCHED** player mood.

GLITCHED has a small chance to occur after successful normal splicing and is guaranteed after activating the Holon Collider.

While GLITCHED:

- One random stat on the player's active Pokémon changes by 1–2 stages every battle turn
- Changes can be positive or negative
- Area music can temporarily speed up or slow down
- Harmless visual corruption can briefly affect the overworld
- A unique GLITCHED mood bubble appears

The integration is completely optional. Mutant Monster Lab functions normally without PokeSurvive installed.

## Compatibility

Designed for **Pokémon Crystal on Gen1Recomp**.

Mutant Monster Lab is designed as a standalone expansion and does not require PokeSurvive or other mods unless using their optional integration features.
