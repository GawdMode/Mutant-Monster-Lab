# Mutant Monster Lab

**Mutant Monster Lab** is a Pokémon Crystal mod for Gen1Recomp that adds an experimental research facility to Cinnabar Island.

Fund the restoration of a questionable Pokémon laboratory, perform increasingly dangerous experiments on individual Pokémon, and eventually bring the **Holon Collider** back online.

## Features

### Five-Tier Lab Progression
Complete successful experiments and bankroll new equipment to advance through five research tiers. Each tier expands what the scientists can do. The final Tier 5 project requires extensive research, a successful Type Splice, and **¥75,000** in funding.

### Pokémon Splicing
Alter individual Pokémon with persistent experimental traits, including:

- Palette altering
- Type splicing
- Stat manipulation
- and more...

Mutations belong to the individual Pokémon and are designed to survive normal play, including PC storage, saving/reloading, and supported evolution paths.

### Fusion Instability
Repeated experimentation carries risk. As stability falls, increasingly severe side effects can occur. Push a specimen far enough and the results can become catastrophic, including Pokémon losing their original form entirely.

### The Holon Collider
Reach Tier 5 to restore the lab's largest experiment.

Each activation rolls **three unique global phenomena** that alter wild Pokémon. The Collider then requires **seven in-game days** to recharge.

Possible phenomena: Level Cascade, Holon Typing, Stat Distortion, Volatile Ecosystem, and more.

Phenomena use weighted rarity: stranger everyday mutations appear more often, while the most catastrophic effects are rarer but always possible.

### Persistent Mutants
Pokémon caught during a Collider event keep their individual mutations even after a later activation changes the world again.

Evolution Cascade also respects mutation inheritance. Species may change while persistent properties such as chromatic palettes, Holon typing, randomized learnsets, Limit Break, Prisma status, Stat Distortion and other lab mutations remain attached to the individual when applicable.

### Cross-Mod Mutation Support
Mutant Monster Lab includes mutation-aware visual compatibility with:

- **PokeSurvive**
- **Angler's Cove Aquarium**
- **Safari Derby**

Cinnabar-created individual palettes take visual priority over PokeSurvive's randomized-type coloring, and supported mutant Pokémon can retain their unusual appearance in Angler's Cove aquariums and Safari Derby races.

All companion mods are optional.

## Installation

Install the mod using the normal Gen1Recomp mod installation process for Pokémon Crystal.
