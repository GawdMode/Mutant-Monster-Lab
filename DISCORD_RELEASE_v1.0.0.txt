🧪 **MUTANT MONSTER LAB v1.0.0 IS HERE!** 🧬

A new experimental research facility has opened on **Cinnabar Island** for Pokémon Crystal on Gen1Recomp, and absolutely none of this feels properly regulated.

💰 **BUILD THE LAB**
Fund **5 research tiers**, complete experiments, unlock new equipment, and help the scientists drag their ruined facility back into operation.

🧬 **MUTATE & SPLICE POKÉMON**
Experiment on individual Pokémon with:
🎨 Chromatic palette mutations
📏 Size changes
🦴 Donor body fragments
🔊 Altered cries
🔀 Type splicing
📊 Stat manipulation
⚠️ Increasingly unstable side effects

Your mutant Pokémon are persistent through **saving, PC storage, and supported evolutions**.

☢️ **FUSION INSTABILITY**
Keep experimenting on the same Pokémon and stability starts dropping. Push things far enough and the failures can get catastrophic. Yes, the scientists probably should have stopped earlier.

⚛️ **THE HOLON COLLIDER**
Reach Tier 5 and restore the lab's ultimate machine.

Every activation rolls **3 unique worldwide phenomena**:

💯 **LEVEL CASCADE** — Wild Pokémon are Lv100
🌀 **HOLON TYPING** — Randomized types
📊 **STAT DISTORTION** — Mutated stat profiles
🧬 **EVOLUTION CASCADE** — Caught Pokémon mutate species every time they level
🚀 **LIMIT BREAK** — Grow beyond Lv100 up to Lv255
🎲 **MOVE COLLAPSE** — Randomized movesets and learnsets
💥 **VOLATILE ECOSYSTEM** — Every wild Pokémon knows EXPLOSION
🌈 **CHROMATIC FIELD** — Persistent randomized palettes
🔒 **LOCKDOWN** — No escaping wild battles
✨ **PRISMA** — Wild Pokémon are shiny
🌎 **WILD SHUFFLE** — Wild encounter species are rerolled

The Collider needs **7 in-game days** to recharge, and Pokémon caught during an event keep their mutations even after the next activation.

🔗 **CROSS-MOD MUTANTS**
Mutation visuals support:
🏕️ PokeSurvive
🐟 Angler's Cove Aquarium
🏇 Safari Derby

So yes, the horrible purple mutant you created in a laboratory can live in your aquarium or become a professional racing athlete.

🧪 **Mutant Monster Lab v1.0.0** is the initial public release. Have fun doing science that should probably require more oversight. ⚡
